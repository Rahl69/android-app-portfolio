Measure Workbench privacy policy

Publisher: zerobsapps
Effective date: October 16, 2026
Contact: jsouthpaww@gmail.com

What stays on your device

Measure Workbench stores your calculations, history, projects, measurements, cut lists, preferences and selected photos in its private storage on your device. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or developer-operated servers. It does not request Internet access or automatically transmit your records to zerobsapps.

Microphone and voice

Microphone permission is optional. When you enable voice entry, the app processes audio on your device with its included speech-recognition model. It does not save microphone recordings or upload audio. Turn voice mode off to stop listening. Voice is stopped when the app leaves the foreground. Spoken feedback is optional and uses the Android text-to-speech service selected on your device. The app requests an offline English voice. The selected speech service is separate software with its own privacy policy.

Photos

You choose photos through Android's file picker or request a photo through a camera app. The selected photo is copied into the project's local storage. Measure Workbench does not scan your photo library. Copies may retain metadata from the original photo. Review the photo before including it in a backup or sharing it.

Sharing, printing and backups

Only actions you initiate send files to another app, printer or storage destination. The destination you select may be a cloud service with its own privacy policy. Project backups can include records and photos. Android's automatic app backup is disabled; use the app's backup feature to keep a copy before changing phones or uninstalling.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can delete projects and individual history entries in the app. Recovery copies may retain the previous saved state for undo and corruption recovery. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes private records and recovery copies. Exported files, backups, printed copies and material shared with other apps remain in their selected locations until you remove them there.

Support

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you send. Do not send sensitive records unless they are needed to explain your request. Contact jsouthpaww@gmail.com to ask about support correspondence or request its deletion.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
